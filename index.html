Questions

Which TVs work with Remote TV?

TVs and streaming devices that run Android TV or Google TV, such as Sony and Philips TVs, Chromecast with Google TV and NVIDIA Shield. Samsung, LG, Roku and Fire TV use their own systems and don't work with Remote TV.

My TV doesn't show up

Make sure your TV is on and that your phone and TV are connected to the same Wi‑Fi network. You can also connect with the TV's IP address: tap “Enter IP address” under the list of TVs.

Why do I have to enter a code?

The first time you connect, your TV shows a code to make sure it's your phone. After that, the TV remembers your phone and connects without a code.

Do casting and mirroring work with my TV?

They need Chromecast built-in, which Google TV and most Android TVs have. Mirroring shows your screen a few seconds behind your phone, so it suits photos and videos better than games, and protected videos from streaming apps show as black.

Which games can I play with the gamepad?

Games on your TV that work with a controller's D-pad and buttons. Turn your phone sideways and choose a D-pad or a stick, with A, B, X and Y, Back, Home and Menu.

Can I connect over Bluetooth?

On Android, yes: Remote TV can also reach your TV over Bluetooth. iPhone can't connect to a TV's remote service over Bluetooth, so on iPhone it works over Wi‑Fi only.

Can I use more than one TV?

The free version works with one TV at a time. To switch to another TV, remove the paired one from the list of TVs. Remote Pro pairs all your TVs.

Is Remote TV free?

Yes, it's free to download and use. You get some free time the first time you connect a TV; when it runs out, watch a short ad for more, or get Remote Pro for unlimited use with no ads, every remote style, your own layouts and all your TVs.

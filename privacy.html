TV Remote Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

TV Remote does not ask you to create an account, and it never sends what you say or type to us. This policy explains what the TV Remote iPhone app (the "App") collects, why, and the choices you have. The App is made by LMK Studio ("we", "us").

The App lets your iPhone control an Android TV or Google TV on the same Wi‑Fi network. Some features use services from Google (Firebase and AdMob), RevenueCat and Apple, described below.

The App collects usage, diagnostic and purchase data so we can keep it working and improve it. None of it includes your name, email address, voice, or anything you type.

Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or Apple Account details.

If you report a problem from Settings, the App opens an email (or the share sheet) with a diagnostic log attached. The log lists the App and device versions, your network type, whether you have Remote Pro, the last TV model you connected to, and recent connection steps. You can read the email before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

What stays on your iPhone and your TV

Voice, typing and your photos go straight from your iPhone to your TV or stay on your iPhone; we never receive them.

  • Voice. When you hold the voice key, the App streams your microphone audio over your local network directly to your TV. The TV and its own assistant process it under the TV maker's and Google's terms.
  • Typing. Text you type with the iPhone keyboard is sent only to your TV.
  • Paired TVs. Each TV's name, local IP address and pairing certificate are saved on your iPhone so it can reconnect.
  • Your photos. Photos you pick as a remote background are copied into the App's storage on your iPhone (up to 3). The App only gets the photos you pick, not your library.
  • Settings and free time. Your remote style, layout and remaining free time are saved on your iPhone.
  • Diagnostic log. A rolling log of connection steps and usage events (up to 256 KB) is kept on your iPhone and leaves it only if you send a problem report.

Deleting the App removes all of this from your iPhone.

Ads and your choices

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for extra free time, and Remote Pro removes them entirely.

  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks you before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not access your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA). Ads may still be chosen using non-personal information such as your country and the App's content.
  • You can turn off personalized ads for all Apple services in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

Retention and security

We keep analytics and crash data only as long as it helps us run and fix the App, and no longer than 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as RevenueCat and Apple need them to provide your purchase and meet legal and tax duties. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the connection to your TV is encrypted with the pairing certificate.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. Because the App has no accounts, most data is linked only to random identifiers; to make a request, email us from Settings > Report a problem so we can find the data linked to your device.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the App.

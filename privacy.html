Remote TV Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Remote TV does not ask you to create an account, and it never sends what you say or type to us. This policy explains what the Remote TV app for iPhone and Android (the "App") collects, why, and the choices you have. The App is made by LMK Studio ("we", "us").

The App lets your phone control an Android TV or Google TV on the same Wi‑Fi network (on Android, also over Bluetooth), cast your photos and videos to it, and mirror your screen. Some features use services from Google (Firebase, AdMob and, on Android, Google Play), the ad networks AdMob works with on iPhone (Meta Audience Network and Liftoff), RevenueCat and Apple, described below. Where the two apps differ, this policy says so.

What stays on your phone and your TV

Voice, typing, the camera, your photos and videos, and your screen go straight from your phone to your TV or stay on your phone; we never receive them.

  • Voice. When you tap the voice key, the App streams your microphone audio over your local network directly to your TV. The TV and its own assistant process it under the TV maker's and Google's terms.
  • Typing. Text you type with your phone's keyboard is sent only to your TV.
  • Camera. When you scan the pairing code, the camera image is read on your phone to find the code, which is then sent only to your TV. No image or video is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Casting. To show the photos and videos you can cast, the App reads your photo library on your phone. A photo or video you cast is served from your phone over your local network to your TV's Chromecast built-in, which plays it; it is never uploaded to us. A photo stored only in iCloud is first downloaded to your iPhone by Apple.
  • Screen mirroring. While you mirror, your screen and its sound are recorded on your phone and streamed over your local network to your TV only. Nothing is saved, and it stops when you end mirroring.
  • Paired TVs. Each TV's name, local IP address (or, on Android, Bluetooth address) and pairing certificate are saved on your phone so it can reconnect.
  • Your photos as backgrounds. Photos you pick as a remote background are copied into the App's storage on your phone (up to 3).
  • Apps, settings and free time. Your list of TV apps, your remote style and layouts, and your remaining free time are saved on your phone.
  • Diagnostic log. A rolling log of connection steps and usage events (up to 256 KB) is kept on your phone and leaves it only if you send a problem report.

When you add an app to your list by name, the App looks it up on Google Play to find its icon and package name; only the name you type is sent. Deleting the App removes all of this from your phone.

Ads and your choices

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for extra free time, and Remote Pro removes them entirely.

  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks you before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, before the first ad, iOS asks whether the App may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google AdMob and its ad partners (Meta Audience Network and Liftoff) may use your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show and measure personalized ads. If you ask the App not to track, the IDFA is not shared, and ads are chosen using non-personal information such as your country and the App's content. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, AdMob may use your Android advertising ID. You can reset it, or delete it to stop personalized ads, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some phones Settings > Google > Ads).
  • AdMob picks which network supplies each ad. The networks receive the data listed above only when an ad is requested or shown.
  • On iPhone, you can turn off personalized ads for all Apple services in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

Retention and security

We keep analytics and crash data only as long as it helps us run and fix the App, and no longer than 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as RevenueCat and Apple or Google need them to provide your purchase and meet legal and tax duties. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the connection to your TV is encrypted with the pairing certificate.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. Because the App has no accounts, most data is linked only to random identifiers; to make a request, email us from Settings > Report a problem so we can find the data linked to your device.

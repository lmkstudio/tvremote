The App and the free plan

The App turns your phone into a remote for Android TV and Google TV devices: over the same Wi‑Fi network, or on Android also over Bluetooth. It can also cast your photos and videos to the TV, mirror your phone's screen, and act as a game controller. We do not guarantee that it works with every TV, every TV software version, or every network. Casting and mirroring need a TV with Chromecast built-in.

  • The free plan gives you a limited amount of use. When it runs out, you can watch a short rewarded ad for more time, or get Remote Pro. The amount of free time per ad and the maximum you can save may change.
  • The free plan pairs one TV at a time.
  • Some remote styles and features are available only with Remote Pro.

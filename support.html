Frequently asked questions

My TV doesn't show up

Make sure your TV is on and that your phone and TV are connected to the same Wi‑Fi network. You can also connect with the TV's IP address: tap Enter IP address under the list of TVs.

Which TVs work with TV Remote?

TVs and streaming devices that run Android TV or Google TV, such as Sony and Philips TVs, Chromecast with Google TV and NVIDIA Shield. Samsung, LG, Roku and Fire TV use their own systems and don't work with TV Remote.

Why do I have to enter a code?

The first time you connect, your TV shows a code to make sure it's your phone. After that, the TV remembers your phone and connects without a code.

Why do I have to pair again?

Your TV forgot this phone, usually after it was reset or updated, or its paired devices were cleared. Tap Pair again and enter the new code shown on your TV.

Where do I find my TV's IP address?

On your TV, open Settings › Network & Internet and select your Wi‑Fi network; the IP address is listed there. On some TVs it's under Settings › Device Preferences › About › Status.

Can I turn my TV on with the app?

Yes, if your TV stays connected to Wi‑Fi in standby. If it's unplugged or its network turns off in standby, turn it on with the TV's remote first.

Why does the app ask for local network access?

TV Remote finds and controls your TV over your Wi‑Fi. Without local network access it can't see your TV. You can turn it on in Settings › TV Remote › Local Network.

Voice search doesn't work

Allow microphone access in Settings › TV Remote. Some TVs don't accept voice from a phone; you can still type with your phone's keyboard.

Can I use more than one TV?

The free version works with one TV at a time. To switch to another TV, remove the paired one from the list of TVs. Remote Pro pairs all your TVs.

Why is the remote locked?

Without Remote Pro, you get some free time the first time you connect a TV. When it runs out, watch a short ad to use it free for a while longer, or get Remote Pro for unlimited use.

What do I get with Remote Pro?

No ads, all your TVs paired at once, and every remote style, including your own photos as the background.

I bought Remote Pro, but the app doesn't show it

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with, then tap Settings › Restore purchase. This also brings Remote Pro to a new phone.

How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions and choose TV Remote to cancel. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I connect over Bluetooth?

No. iPhone can't connect to a TV's remote service over Bluetooth, so TV Remote works over Wi‑Fi only.

Frequently asked questions

My TV doesn't show up

Make sure your TV is on and that your phone and TV are connected to the same Wi‑Fi network. You can also connect with the TV's IP address: tap Enter IP address under the list of TVs.

Which TVs work with Remote TV?

TVs and streaming devices that run Android TV or Google TV, such as Sony and Philips TVs, Chromecast with Google TV and NVIDIA Shield. Samsung, LG, Roku and Fire TV use their own systems and don't work with Remote TV.

Why do I have to enter a code?

The first time you connect, your TV shows a code to make sure it's your phone. After that, the TV remembers your phone and connects without a code.

Why do I have to pair again?

Your TV forgot this phone, usually after it was reset or updated, or its paired devices were cleared. Tap Pair again and enter the new code shown on your TV.

Where do I find my TV's IP address?

On your TV, open Settings › Network & Internet and select your Wi‑Fi network; the IP address is listed there. On some TVs it's under Settings › Device Preferences › About › Status.

Can I turn my TV on with the app?

Yes, if your TV stays connected to Wi‑Fi in standby. If it's unplugged or its network turns off in standby, turn it on with the TV's remote first.

Why does the app ask for local network access?

On iPhone, Remote TV finds and controls your TV over your Wi‑Fi, which needs local network access; without it the app can't see your TV. You can turn it on in Settings › Remote TV › Local Network. Android doesn't ask for this.

Voice search doesn't work

Allow microphone access for Remote TV in your phone's settings (on iPhone, Settings › Remote TV; on Android, Settings › Apps › Remote TV › Permissions). Some TVs don't accept voice from a phone; you can still type with your phone's keyboard.

Can I use more than one TV?

The free version works with one TV at a time. To switch to another TV, remove the paired one from the list of TVs. Remote Pro pairs all your TVs.

Why is the remote locked?

Without Remote Pro, you get some free time the first time you connect a TV. When it runs out, watch a short ad to use it free for a while longer, or get Remote Pro for unlimited use.

What do I get with Remote Pro?

No ads, all your TVs paired at once, every remote style including your own photos as the background, and your own layouts.

I bought Remote Pro, but the app doesn't show it

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account or Google account you bought it with, then tap Settings › Restore purchase. This also brings Remote Pro to a new phone. A purchase made on iPhone works on Apple devices, and one made on Android works on Android devices.

How do I cancel my subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from.

iPhone: open Settings › your name › Subscriptions and choose Remote TV to cancel. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions and choose Remote TV. For a refund, follow Google Play's refund steps.

Can I connect over Bluetooth?

On Android, yes: Remote TV can also find and pair your TV over Bluetooth. On iPhone, no: iPhone can't connect to a TV's remote service over Bluetooth, so it works over Wi‑Fi only.

Casting or mirroring doesn't work

Casting photos and videos and mirroring your screen need a TV with Chromecast built-in, which Google TV and most Android TVs have, on the same Wi‑Fi as your phone. To cast, allow access to your photos. Mirroring shows your screen a few seconds behind your phone, so it suits photos and videos better than games, and protected videos from streaming apps show as black.

Which games can I play with the gamepad?

Games on your TV that work with a controller's D-pad and buttons. Tap the gamepad key at the top left of the remote and turn your phone sideways.
